# Added practice skills

All previously suggested skills are now available:

- Divide by 6: divide by 3, then halve (or reverse).
- Multiply by 9 and 11: multiply by 10, then subtract/add the original number.
- Divide by 0.5 and 0.25: double or multiply by 4.
- Find 75%: subtract one quarter from the whole.
- Increase/decrease by 10%, 20% and 25%: calculate the final total.

Each has independent progress and three difficulty levels. Percentage changes are included in the percentages preset and can be selected separately in a custom mix. Existing custom selections stay unchanged.
